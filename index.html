FIELD MANUAL Controls & weapons ↓

01   Get in position
Left/Right move. Enter jumps forward; Backspace jumps up, double-tap it to backflip. Touch buttons do the same.

02   Line up your shot
Up/Down or W/S aim. On touch, drag to aim or use the angle slider. Judge the arc yourself; wind bends rockets but not grenades.

03   Make a mess
Hold Space or the fire button to charge; release to shoot. You get 3 seconds to retreat. Grenade and sticky fuse: keys 1–5 or the selector. F1/F2 select bazooka/grenade; F3 selects a heavy mortar, F4 a sticky bomb. Grenades, mortars and stickies are limited. Falling can hurt you; water is lethal, and from round six the sea climbs every round. Solo skill is chosen in the menu.

04   Field notes
Grab a supply crate to heal or restock. Rain and storms strengthen the wind. Choose single or best-of-three, and try the date-seeded Daily Island; your career record stays on this device.
