FIELD MANUAL Controls & weapons ↓

01   Get in position
Left/Right move. Enter jumps forward; Backspace jumps up, double-tap it to backflip. Tab or the ⇥ WORM button chooses your worm; SKIP passes the turn. Touch buttons do the same. Delete or NAMES cycles team/name/health labels. Shift+Delete keeps team energy bars visible.

02   Line up your shot
Up/Down or W/S aim. On desktop, move the mouse to a battlefield edge or drag with the middle mouse button to look around; left-click to aim. On touch, drag the battlefield to look around; tap to aim or use the angle slider. Judge the arc yourself; wind bends rockets but not grenades. Open ＋ SPECIAL for shotgun, dynamite, cluster, air strike, petrol bomb, bat, homing and holy grenades. Air strike asks you to click a target.

03   Make a mess
Hold Space or the fire button to charge; release to shoot. You get 3 seconds to retreat. Grenade and sticky fuse: keys 1–5 or the selector. F1/F2 select bazooka/grenade; F3 selects a heavy mortar, F4 a sticky bomb; M places a proximity mine. Grenades, mortars, stickies and mines are limited. Falling can hurt you; water is lethal, and from round six a chosen sudden-death event fires. Open ✦ UTILS for rope, jet pack, parachute, teleport, girder and freeze. Solo skill is chosen in the menu.

04   Field notes
Walk onto an airdropped supply crate after it lands to heal, restock weapons or add a field-kit charge. Choose a scheme preset, turn/retreat pace and sudden death, or tune them individually. Rain and storms strengthen the wind. Try the date-seeded Daily Island; your career record stays on this device. Replay the last shot with ↺ and share a result card from the menu. After the first load the game also works offline.
